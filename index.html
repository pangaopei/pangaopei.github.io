THEORETICAL CONDENSED MATTER PHYSICS

Gaopei Pan潘高培

Quantum matter through models and computation.

I am a theoretical condensed matter physicist, currently visiting Prof. Fakher F. Assaad’s group at the University of Würzburg through October 2026, following a postdoctoral position in the same group from 2024 to April 2026. In November 2026, I will join Prof. Xueyang Song’s group at HKUST as a postdoctoral researcher. I received my Ph.D. from the Institute of Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, in 2023, under the supervision of Prof. Zi Yang Meng.

I develop models and use determinant quantum Monte Carlo (DQMC) and projector quantum Monte Carlo (PQMC) to study the equilibrium properties of strongly correlated quantum matter, at finite temperature and in the ground state. My research spans quantum criticality and non-Fermi liquids, moiré flat bands, Kondo systems, and lattice gauge models.

My main interest is using quantum Monte Carlo to explore novel quantum phases and uncover the physical mechanisms behind their emergence and transitions. I combine model design with numerical simulations, developing and adapting computational methods as needed to address these questions.
